## Overarching (owner, verbatim in substance)
- REALLY well done; these are tweaks to a superb design.
- Bandwidth is too important to be so subtle. Show incoming AND outgoing, and whether it is strained (insufficient for the task, limiting it). Show it within the shape; pixels flying in from outside the image are confusing and busy.
- System strain must not look anything like needs-you. Use the reddish glow behind/inside the shape (as in the first GPT render). The dis-alignment of the shape, as if noise were added, is very good: keep it.
- Play: just a brief motion between them; no apparent data exchange or interaction that looks like it means something.
- Needs more thought: universal ways to represent memory, CPU, background processes and bandwidth need (and whether it strains the system).
