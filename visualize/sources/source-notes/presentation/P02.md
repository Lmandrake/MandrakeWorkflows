# P02: hoffler2007

- **Citation (verified at fetch time):** Hoffler, T. N., Leutner, D. (2007). Instructional animation versus static pictures: A meta-analysis. Learning and Instruction 17(6), 722-738.
- **Tier:** A | **Status:** reference_only
- **URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475207001077
- **Access:** ERIC abstract record; Elsevier-paywalled full text
- **Files:** `p02_hoffler2007_fetch_https-eric.ed.gov-idEJ780451.txt`

**What it claims about animation/slides:** Instructional animations show a medium overall learning advantage over static pictures; larger when animation is representational rather than decorational, highly realistic, or teaching procedural-motor knowledge.

**Evidence / n:** Meta-analysis of 26 primary studies, 76 pairwise comparisons; mean weighted d = 0.37 (95% CI 0.25-0.49); representational d = 0.40; video-based d = 0.76; procedural-motor d = 1.06. Learning (not data-chart) context.
