**What it claims about animation/slides:** Proposes a dual-channel, limited-capacity, active-processing theory of multimedia learning, identifies five overload scenarios, and gives nine theory-based techniques (e.g., segmenting, pre-training, modality, signaling) with the supporting experiments.

**Evidence / n:** Synthesis of the authors' own experiments (various small lab studies on narrated animation); no single n. Theory plus summarised effects.
