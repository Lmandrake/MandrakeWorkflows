# P04: heer_robertson2007

- **Citation (verified at fetch time):** Heer, J., Robertson, G. (2007). Animated transitions in statistical data graphics. IEEE TVCG 13(6), 1240-1247 (InfoVis 2007).
- **Tier:** A | **Status:** acquired
- **URL:** https://idl.uw.edu/papers/animated-transitions
- **Access:** Lab-hosted PDF (UW IDL)
- **Files:** `p04_heer_robertson2007_heer2007.pdf`, `p04_heer_robertson2007_heer2007.pdf.txt`

**What it claims about animation/slides:** Extends a model of data graphics to transitions, gives a taxonomy of transition types and design principles (DynaVis), and reports that animated transitions can improve graphical perception, with staged and congruent transitions helping estimation of changes.

**Evidence / n:** Two controlled experiments, 24 participants (balanced by age, gender, profession).
