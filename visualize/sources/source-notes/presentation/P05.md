# P05: robertson2008

- **Citation (verified at fetch time):** Robertson, G., Fernandez, R., Fisher, D., Lee, B., Stasko, J. (2008). Effectiveness of animation in trend visualization. IEEE TVCG 14(6), 1325-1332.
- **Tier:** A | **Status:** acquired
- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/effectiveness-of-animation-in-trend-visualization
- **Access:** Publisher-hosted PDF on Microsoft Research site
- **Files:** `p05_robertson2008.pdf`, `p05_robertson2008.pdf.txt`

**What it claims about animation/slides:** Compares animated trend display (Gapminder-style) with overlaid trace and small-multiples static displays for analysis and presentation. Animation was fastest for presentation and enjoyed but error-prone; slowest and least effective for analysis; small multiples most accurate.

**Evidence / n:** Two user studies, 18 participants each (analysis study and presentation study).
