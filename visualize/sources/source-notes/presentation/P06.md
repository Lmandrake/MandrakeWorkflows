# P06: dragicevic2011

- **Citation (verified at fetch time):** Dragicevic, P., Bezerianos, A., Javed, W., Elmqvist, N., Fekete, J.-D. (2011). Temporal distortion for animated transitions. CHI 2011, 2009-2018.
- **Tier:** A | **Status:** acquired
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/1978942.1979233
- **Access:** Author-hosted PDF recovered via HAL/author site
- **Files:** `p06_dragicevic2011.pdf`, `p06_dragicevic2011.pdf.txt`

**What it claims about animation/slides:** Tests constant speed, slow-in/slow-out, fast-in/fast-out and an adaptive technique for object tracking in animated transitions; slow-in/slow-out outperformed the others, with differences by transition type.

**Evidence / n:** Controlled experiment, 12 paid students; 4 techniques x 2 datasets (generated, real); about 45 minutes each.
