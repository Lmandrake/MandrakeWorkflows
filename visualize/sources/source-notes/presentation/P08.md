# P08: chalbi2019

- **Citation (verified at fetch time):** Chalbi, A., Ritchie, J., Park, D., Choi, J., Roussel, N., Elmqvist, N., Chevalier, F. (2020; presented VIS 2019). Common fate for animated transitions in visualization. IEEE TVCG 26(1), 386-396. (Brief gave 2019; journal issue is 2020.)
- **Tier:** A | **Status:** acquired
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2019.2934288
- **Access:** Author-hosted PDF (Aarhus, Elmqvist)
- **Files:** `p08_chalbi2019.pdf`, `p08_chalbi2019.pdf.txt`

**What it claims about animation/slides:** Gestalt common fate extends beyond velocity to coordinated luminance and size changes; ranks grouping strength: motion > dynamic luminance, size, luminance; dynamic size > dynamic luminance, position; dynamic luminance > size, with dependence on visualization context.

**Evidence / n:** Crowdsourced graphical-perception study (Mechanical Turk, 42 trials per participant) plus follow-up with 10 in-lab participants on a Gapminder-like scatterplot and election map.
