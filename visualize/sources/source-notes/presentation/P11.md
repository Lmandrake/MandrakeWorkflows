# P11: hullman2013

- **Citation (verified at fetch time):** Hullman, J., Drucker, S., Henry Riche, N., Lee, B., Fisher, D., Adar, E. (2013). A deeper understanding of sequence in narrative visualization. IEEE TVCG 19(12), 2406-2415.
- **Tier:** A | **Status:** acquired
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2013.119
- **Access:** Author-hosted PDF (Northwestern)
- **Files:** `p11_hullman2013.pdf`, `p11_hullman2013.pdf.txt`

**What it claims about animation/slides:** Characterises sequencing in slideshow-style narrative visualization, proposes a graph-driven cost model of transitions between visualizations, ranks transition types by audience cost, and reports memory and preference benefits for some sequence structures.

**Evidence / n:** Qualitative analysis of 42 professional narrative visualizations; validation studies including a crowd study with 82 participants.
