**What it claims about animation/slides:** Catalogues the constituents of data videos (visualization types, attention cues, transitions, narrative structure) and derives authoring-tool implications from storyteller workshops.

**Evidence / n:** Qualitative analysis of 50 professionally designed data videos plus workshops with experienced storytellers (cinematography, graphic design, screenwriting).
