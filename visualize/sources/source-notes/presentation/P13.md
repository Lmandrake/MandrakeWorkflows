# P13: mckenna2017

- **Citation (verified at fetch time):** McKenna, S., Henry Riche, N., Lee, B., Boy, J., Meyer, M. (2017). Visual narrative flow: exploring factors shaping data visualization story reading experiences. Computer Graphics Forum 36(3), 377-387.
- **Tier:** A | **Status:** acquired
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.13195
- **Access:** Author-hosted PDF (Utah VDL)
- **Files:** `p13_mckenna2017.pdf`, `p13_mckenna2017.pdf.txt`

**What it claims about animation/slides:** Identifies seven flow-factors of data stories (navigation input, level of control, progress, layout, role of visualization, progression, feedback); finds visuals and navigation feedback (e.g., static vs animated transitions) affect engagement while discrete vs continuous control may not.

**Evidence / n:** 80 existing stories analysed; two exploratory stepper-vs-scroller studies; crowdsourced study with 240 participants.
