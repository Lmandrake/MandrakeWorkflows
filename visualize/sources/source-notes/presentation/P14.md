**What it claims about animation/slides:** Gemini is a declarative grammar for staged, timed transitions plus a recommender; Gemini2 splits a complex change into semantic edit operations and recommends staged keyframes.

**Evidence / n:** Gemini2 ranking study with 50 Mechanical Turk participants (35 male, 15 female); Gemini is a system and grammar paper with examples.
