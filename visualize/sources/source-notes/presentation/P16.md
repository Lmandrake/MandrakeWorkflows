**What it claims about animation/slides:** Slides built on assertion-evidence principles produced better comprehension and fewer misconceptions than default PowerPoint bullet slides.

**Evidence / n:** Between-subjects comparison, n = 110 engineering students, essay-response scoring. Slide conditions differ on six principles at once, so single-factor attribution is not possible. Static slides; no animation tested.
