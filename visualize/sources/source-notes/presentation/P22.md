**What it says about motion:** Morph is a slide transition that animates objects common to two consecutive slides (text, shapes, pictures, SmartArt, WordArt; charts do not morph). Set up by duplicating a slide and moving/resizing/recoloring objects on the second, then Transitions > Morph; Effect Options choose by Objects, Words or Characters. Tips page covers naming objects (!!name) to pair them, motion-path and rotation effects. Animation vs transition: an animation applies to one element (entrance, exit, emphasis, motion path; many per slide); a transition applies to a whole slide (one per slide); Morph is a transition that looks like an animation. Page says effects 'when used carefully, can help communicate your message well'. **Durations, easing, reduced motion: not specified** in the captured pages (duration is a user setting on the Transitions tab).
